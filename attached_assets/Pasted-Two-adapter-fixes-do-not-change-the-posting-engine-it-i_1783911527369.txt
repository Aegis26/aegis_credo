Two adapter fixes (do not change the posting engine — it is behaving correctly).

FIX 1 — Zero-balance accounts break the import.
Clicking "Import & reconcile" on the real Craig's QBO Trial Balance CSV fails with:
  HTTP 400: lines[8] must have exactly one of a positive debit or credit amount
Cause: QuickBooks lists accounts with a ZERO/blank balance in the trial balance
(e.g. "Arizona Dept. of Revenue Payable" has neither a debit nor a credit). The
adapter is passing these through as journal lines with no amount, and the posting
service correctly rejects them.
Fix: in the adapter, still UPSERT zero-balance accounts into the chart of accounts
(the user should keep them), but EXCLUDE them from the opening journal entry —
they contribute nothing and must not become journal lines. Add a test using the
real Craig's file asserting the import succeeds and that zero-balance accounts
exist in the COA but produce no journal lines.

FIX 2 — The account-type resolver has a bad default and mis-types clear expenses.
On the real file it defaulted these to Asset:
  - Equipment Rental (an EXPENSE)
  - Maintenance and Repair:Equipment Repairs (an EXPENSE — note its parent
    "Maintenance and Repair" correctly resolved to Expense)
  - The unresolved ones (Office Expenses, Job Expenses, Miscellaneous,
    Discounts given) all default to "Asset" in the dropdown.
Fixes:
  - Do NOT default unresolved accounts to Asset. Either leave the dropdown empty
    and force an explicit choice, or inherit from the QuickBooks parent:child
    hierarchy (colon-delimited) — a child should inherit its parent's type.
  - Strengthen the name-based resolver: names containing "Expense(s)", "Repairs",
    "Rental", "Fees", "Maintenance" resolve to Expense.
  - Treat "Discounts given" style accounts as contra-revenue and surface them for
    confirmation rather than typing them Asset.
  - Unit-test each case against the real Craig's file.

CRITICAL: the trial balance BALANCES even when account types are wrong, so
balancing is not proof of correctness. Tests must assert account TYPE, not just
that debits equal credits.
